dataColors[0-7] in a Power BI theme is the fixed 8-color palette Power BI cycles through for every categorical visual. Slot 1-3 get used first — always carry your strongest brand colors there. Slots 4-8 fill out charts with more categories and should still pass WCAG contrast against white/dark backgrounds (bibb.pro flags this for you before you copy the hex codes).
